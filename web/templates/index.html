Upload an object

Send a closed (watertight) .obj model. We treat it as a hollow metal cavity and compute its {{ 20 }} lowest optical modes.

The refractive index is the medium filling your object: 1.0 for air or vacuum, about 1.5 for glass. More modes means a finer mesh and a longer solve; the mesh grows in proportion to this number.

Leave this on auto for the default resolution, which is set by the physics of the modes you asked for. If your object has fine detail the modes depend on — a thin wall, a narrow gap — enter its size in millimetres here and the mesh will be refined to resolve it. Setting it larger than the automatic value has no effect: the mesh is never made coarser than the detail you name.
